Introduction

The Claude Certified Associate – Foundations certification validates that an individual can apply Claude to complete business and productivity tasks with minimal guidance. This includes using built-in platform features, capabilities, and tools to streamline workflows; identifying opportunities to improve processes with Claude; selecting approaches that balance quality, efficiency, and cost; and recognizing limitations and escalating more complex or technical work to Claude Architects and Developers.

This guide is the study companion to the official Exam Guide, which is the authoritative reference for the credential: its purpose, intended audience, exam format and content blueprint, scoring, and policies.

Target Candidate

The certification is intended for professionals who use Claude as a productivity tool and build Claude Projects in their day-to-day roles. They operate across functions such as operations, marketing, project management, education, communications, and general knowledge work, applying AI to improve efficiency, decision-making, and content development. The audience includes both internal staff who maintain and optimize ongoing AI-enabled workflows and external consultants who support implementation, use-case identification, and process redesign.

The exam targets the minimally qualified candidate (MQC): a professional who uses Claude as a core productivity tool and can apply it effectively within real-world workflows to improve efficiency, quality, and outcomes. The MQC has foundational, applied knowledge of Claude's capabilities, including prompt structuring, task orchestration, and familiarity with features such as Projects, Artifacts, and workflow-based interactions. Candidates generally have limited to moderate technical expertise: they are positioned between casual AI prompt users and technical AI practitioners. Recommended experience (recommended, not required — there are no mandatory prerequisites, and no software-development or API experience is needed):

  • Regular, hands-on experience using Claude in a professional setting
  • A foundational understanding of structured problem-solving, workflow design, and digital tool usage
  • Experience in roles such as business analyst, project manager, operations lead, marketing/communications/HR/education professional, consultant, or knowledge worker
  • A practical understanding of AI limitations, including hallucinations, context constraints, and data sensitivity

This certification is not intended for software developers who build against APIs or design agentic systems, nor for specialists in machine learning or advanced AI system design; that scope belongs to the Claude Architect and Claude Developer credentials.

目标考生（Target Candidate）

本认证面向在日常工作中把 Claude 当作生产力工具使用、并构建 Claude Projects 的专业人士。他们跨越运营、市场营销、项目管理、教育、传播与一般知识工作等职能，运用 AI 来提升效率、决策质量与内容开发水平。受众既包括维护并优化持续运行的 AI 赋能工作流的内部员工，也包括支持实施、用例识别与流程再设计的外部顾问。

  • 担任过业务分析师、项目经理、运营负责人、市场营销/传播/人力资源/教育专业人士、顾问或知识工作者等角色的经验

本认证不面向针对 API 构建应用或设计智能体系统（agentic systems）的软件开发者，也不面向机器学习或高级 AI 系统设计方面的专家；那一范围属于 Claude 架构师（Claude Architect）与 Claude 开发者（Claude Developer）证书。

1.1 Create effective prompts for business and technical tasks

Official wording: "Claude responds well to clear, explicit instructions. Being specific about the output you want can help enhance results." — Prompting best practices, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)

Key knowledge:

  • Be clear and direct: state the task, the audience, and the desired output explicitly, rather than relying on Claude to infer what you meant from a vague prompt.
  • Add context to improve performance: explain why the work matters and what goal it serves, so Claude can generalise from that motivation to more targeted responses.
  • Be specific about the output format and constraints you want, and give instructions as sequential steps in numbered lists or bullet points when order or completeness matters.
  • Use examples effectively: a few well-crafted examples — few-shot or multishot prompting — steer format, tone, and structure, and should be relevant, diverse, and structured.
  • Structure complex prompts with XML tags and give Claude a role where that helps focus its behaviour and tone for the use case.

Key skills:

  • Write a prompt that a colleague with minimal context could follow without asking what you meant.
  • Specify format, length, tone, audience, and constraints in the prompt itself, for both everyday business writing and more technical requests.
  • Add one to a few relevant examples that show the exact output pattern you want repeated.

  • 写出的提示要让一位几乎没有背景信息的同事读了也不必反问你是什么意思。
  • 在提示本身中写明格式、长度、语气、受众与约束，既适用于日常业务写作，也适用于更技术性的请求。
  • 加入一到几个相关示例，展示你希望被复现的确切输出模式。

1.2 Apply task decomposition techniques to structure complex requests

Official wording: "Explicit prompt chaining (breaking a task into sequential API calls) is still useful when you need to inspect intermediate outputs or enforce a specific pipeline structure." — Prompting best practices, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)

Key knowledge:

  • Task decomposition means breaking a complex request into smaller, focused subtasks, instead of asking for everything in one overloaded prompt where steps can be dropped.
  • Prompt chaining connects those subtasks in sequence, so each step works on the output of the previous step and intermediate outputs can be inspected before you continue.
  • Claude also handles long-horizon work incrementally, making steady advances on a few things at a time rather than attempting everything at once.
  • For long or data-rich work, put long-form data at the top of the prompt and structure documents and metadata with XML tags, so each subtask has the context it needs.
  • Use structured formats for state data, such as task status or results tracking, and unstructured text for general progress notes.

Key skills:

  • Split a large request — for example research, then outline, then draft, then edit — into an ordered chain of single-purpose prompts.
  • Define what a successful output looks like for each subtask before moving to the next one.
  • Check the intermediate output at each step and correct direction early, rather than only at the end.

1.2 运用任务拆解技术组织复杂请求

EN: Apply task decomposition techniques to structure complex requests

官方原文： "Explicit prompt chaining (breaking a task into sequential API calls) is still useful when you need to inspect intermediate outputs or enforce a specific pipeline structure." — Prompting best practices, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)

关键知识：

  • 任务拆解（task decomposition）是指把一个复杂请求拆成更小、更聚焦的子任务，而不是把所有东西塞进一个过载的提示里、导致步骤被遗漏。
  • 提示链（prompt chaining）把这些子任务按顺序连接起来，使每一步都作用于上一步的输出，且中间输出可以在继续之前被检查。
  • Claude 也能以增量方式处理长周期（long-horizon）工作：一次稳步推进少数几件事，而不是试图同时做完所有事。
  • 对于篇幅长或数据丰富的工作，把长篇数据放在提示的顶部，并用 XML 标签组织文档与元数据，使每个子任务都拥有它所需的上下文。
  • 对状态数据（如任务状态或结果跟踪）使用结构化格式，对一般进度说明使用非结构化文本。

关键技能：

  • 把一个大请求 —— 例如先研究、再列提纲、再起草、再编辑 —— 拆成一条由单一用途提示组成的有序链条。
  • 在进入下一个子任务之前，先定义每个子任务的成功输出是什么样子。
  • 在每一步检查中间输出并尽早纠正方向，而不是只在最后才检查。

1.3 Iterate prompts to improve output quality

Official wording: "Ask Claude to self-check. Append something like “Before you finish, verify your answer against [test criteria].” This catches errors reliably, especially for coding and math." — Prompting best practices, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)

Key knowledge:

  • Iteration is a normal part of prompting: generate a draft, review it against clear criteria, and refine it, rather than expecting the first output to be final.
  • The most common chaining pattern for improvement is self-correction: generate a draft, have Claude review it against criteria, then have Claude refine it based on that review.
  • Prefer general instructions over overly prescriptive steps when asking Claude to think a problem through, because a prompt such as “think thoroughly” often produces better reasoning.
  • If a prompt is producing the wrong kind of result, change the instruction itself — for example, tell Claude what to do instead of only what not to do — rather than simply repeating the same prompt.
  • Worked examples in a prompt shape how Claude approaches similar problems, so improving the examples is one way to improve later outputs.

Key skills:

  • Diagnose what is actually wrong with an output — missing content, wrong tone, wrong format, or a factual gap — and name that problem in the follow-up prompt.
  • Run a draft → review against criteria → refine cycle, and keep each revision focused on a specific improvement.
  • Ask Claude to self-check its answer against criteria you supply before you accept the final version.

1.3 迭代提示以改进输出质量

EN: Iterate prompts to improve output quality

官方原文： "Ask Claude to self-check. Append something like “Before you finish, verify your answer against [test criteria].” This catches errors reliably, especially for coding and math." — Prompting best practices, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)

关键知识：

  • 迭代是提示的正常组成部分：生成草稿、对照明确的标准审核、再加以完善，而不是期望第一次输出就是最终版。
  • 最常见的改进链模式是自我纠正：生成草稿、让 Claude 对照标准审核草稿、再让 Claude 根据审核结果完善草稿。
  • 在让 Claude 思考一个问题时，优先给出一般性指令而非过度规定步骤，因为像「彻底思考」这样的提示往往能产生更好的推理。
  • 如果一个提示产生的是错误类型的结果，就修改指令本身 —— 例如告诉 Claude 应该做什么，而不只是不应该做什么 —— 而不是简单地重复同一个提示。
  • 提示中的示例会塑造 Claude 处理类似问题的方式，因此改进示例是改进后续输出的一种方法。

关键技能：

  • 诊断输出到底哪里出了问题 —— 内容缺失、语气不对、格式不对，还是事实缺口 —— 并在后续提示中点明那个问题。
  • 运行「草稿 → 对照标准审核 → 完善」的循环，并让每一次修订都聚焦于一项具体的改进。
  • 在接受最终版本之前，让 Claude 对照你提供的标准自查它的答案。

1.4 Adapt prompting strategies based on task type (analysis, research, drafting, brainstorming)

Official wording: "With research, Claude investigates your question in depth. It runs multiple searches that build on each other and decides what to look into next based on what it finds." — Use research on Claude, Anthropic (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11088861-use-research-on-claude)

Key knowledge:

  • Different task types need different prompts: analysis benefits from clear success criteria and step-by-step thinking, drafting benefits from audience, tone, and format instructions, and brainstorming benefits from a deliberately open brief.
  • For research tasks, provide clear success criteria, encourage source verification across multiple sources, and use a structured approach for complex questions.
  • Research mode is a distinct strategy, not just a longer prompt: Claude explores different angles of the question and works through open questions systematically, searching the web and connected tools.
  • For long document analysis, ground responses in quotes: ask Claude to quote relevant parts of the documents first before carrying out its task.
  • Giving Claude a role focuses its behaviour and tone for the use case, which matters more for some tasks — such as drafting in a particular voice — than for others.

Key skills:

  • Choose the prompting approach to match the task: criteria and verification for analysis and research, examples and tone for drafting, breadth and quantity for brainstorming.
  • Write a research brief that states the question, the angles to explore, and what a successful answer must contain.
  • Decide when a task should stay in ordinary chat and when it should be escalated to research mode.

1.4 根据任务类型（分析、研究、起草、头脑风暴）调整提示策略

关键知识：

  • 不同任务类型需要不同的提示：分析受益于明确的成功标准与逐步思考，起草受益于受众、语气与格式方面的指令，头脑风暴受益于刻意开放的简报。

  • 研究模式（research mode）是一种独立的策略，而不只是一个更长的提示：Claude 会探索问题的不同角度，系统地处理开放问题，并搜索网络与已连接的工具。

  • 选择与任务相匹配的提示方法：分析与研究用标准与核实，起草用示例与语气，头脑风暴重广度与数量。
  • 写一份研究简报，说明问题、要探索的角度，以及一个成功的答案必须包含什么。
  • 判断一个任务应当留在普通聊天中，还是应当升级到研究模式（research mode）。

2.1 Evaluate Claude-generated outputs for accuracy and completeness

Official wording: "Users should not rely on Claude as a singular source of truth and should carefully scrutinize any high-stakes advice given by Claude." — Claude is providing incorrect or misleading responses. What's going on?, Anthropic (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8525154-claude-is-providing-incorrect-or-misleading-responses-what-s-going-on)

Key knowledge:

  • Evaluating an output means checking two different things: accuracy — are the facts, figures, names, and citations correct — and completeness — does it cover everything the original request asked for.
  • Claude's confidence is not evidence of accuracy: an output can be fluent, well structured, and still wrong, so evaluation cannot stop at how convincing it sounds.
  • Knowledge limits matter for evaluation: each model has a knowledge cutoff, and models may not have accurate information about events after that date unless current sources are supplied or searched.
  • Asking Claude to self-check against criteria you provide is a useful first evaluation step, but it does not replace checking against the original source material.
  • Completeness can be checked directly against the prompt: list what was requested, then confirm each item is present in the output.

Key skills:

  • Compare an output line by line against the source documents or data it was supposed to be based on.
  • Check names, dates, numbers, and totals separately from the surrounding prose.
  • Mark anything you could not verify, rather than passing the output on as if it had all been checked.

Key knowledge:

  • Producing incorrect or misleading responses is known as “hallucinating” information, and it is a byproduct of current limitations of frontier generative AI models, including Claude.

2.3 Apply fact-checking and validation techniques

Official wording: "When working with web search results, users should review Claude's cited sources. What's going on?, Anthropic (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8525154-claude-is-providing-incorrect-or-misleading-responses-what-s-going-on)

Key knowledge:

  • Fact-checking starts at the cited source: open the original website or document, because it may contain context or details that Claude's synthesis left out or misinterpreted.
  • The quality of Claude's responses depends on the underlying sources it references, so a weak or outdated source produces a weak answer even when the writing is good.
  • Verification across multiple sources is an official research practice: ask Claude to verify information across multiple sources, and compare what independent sources say.

  • Open and read at least the key cited sources behind a factual output, rather than trusting the citation list alone.
  • Triangulate an important claim across two or more independent, authoritative sources before using it.
  • Recalculate totals and re-check dates and figures yourself for anything that will be quoted, published, or sent on.

3.4 Understand and manage context limitations and memory considerations (when to restart, summarize, or persist)

Official wording: "The “context window” refers to all the text a language model can reference when generating a response, including the response itself." — Context windows, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/context-windows)

Key knowledge:

  • The context window is everything Claude can reference in the current conversation — your messages, Claude's replies, and uploaded content accumulate turn by turn. Context window size varies by model and plan (for example, 200K tokens on models outside the current large-window lineup on paid plans, up to 1M tokens for current models), so very long chats eventually approach the limit.
  • More context is not automatically better: as token count grows, accuracy and recall degrade — a phenomenon the platform docs call context rot. Long, unfocused conversations can quietly get worse before they hit any hard limit.
  • Automatic context management: for users on paid plans with code execution enabled, when a conversation approaches the context window limit, Claude summarizes earlier messages to make room for new content, allowing conversations to continue in most cases; the full chat history is preserved so Claude can reference it, even after earlier portions have been summarized. Longer conversations that trigger this use more of your usage limit.
  • Memory is separate from the context window. Claude saves memory as a set of individual topics as you chat, rather than summarizing conversations after they end, and carries that context into new conversations — with memory, Claude transforms from a stateless chat interface into a knowledgeable collaborator that builds understanding over time. You can view, edit, or delete memory topics in Settings, tell Claude to "remember this," or turn memory off for your account or for a single chat.
  • Project memory is scoped: each project has its own memory, and while working in a project Claude uses the project's memory alongside your memory from chats outside of projects, but not memory from other projects.
  • Persist vs. restart: durable context belongs in a Project's knowledge base and instructions or in memory topics; a single chat's context window is temporary. Incognito chats and chats with memory turned off are not saved to memory.

Key skills:

  • Decide when to restart: start a fresh chat when the topic changes, when the conversation has drifted, or when recall of early details starts slipping (context rot).
  • Decide when to summarize: ask Claude for a concise handoff summary of decisions, facts, and open items, then paste it into a new chat or save it into a Project to continue without the accumulated bulk.
  • Decide what to persist: put stable facts, preferences, and reference files into memory or a Project's knowledge base and instructions instead of relying on one long conversation to hold them.
  • Periodically review and edit saved memory topics so outdated facts (for example, a moved deadline) do not keep shaping new conversations.

3.4 理解并管理上下文局限与记忆方面的考量（何时重启、何时总结、何时持久保存）

EN: Understand and manage context limitations and memory considerations (when to restart, summarize, or persist)

官方原文： "The “context window” refers to all the text a language model can reference when generating a response, including the response itself." — Context windows, Anthropic (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/context-windows)

关键知识：

  • 上下文窗口（context window）是 Claude 在当前对话中可以引用的一切 —— 你的消息、Claude 的回复与上传的内容逐轮累积。上下文窗口的大小因模型与方案而异（例如，付费方案上当前大窗口阵容之外的模型为 200K tokens，当前模型最高可达 1M tokens），因此非常长的聊天最终会接近限额。
  • 上下文越多并不自动越好：随着 token 数量增长，准确性与回忆能力会下降 —— 平台文档把这一现象称为上下文腐化（context rot）。冗长、缺乏焦点的对话可能在触及任何硬性限额之前就悄悄变差。
  • 自动上下文管理：对于启用了代码执行的付费方案用户，当一次对话接近上下文窗口限额时，Claude 会总结较早的消息以腾出空间容纳新内容，使对话在大多数情况下可以继续；完整的聊天历史会被保留，因此即使较早的部分已被总结，Claude 仍可引用它。触发这一机制的较长对话会消耗更多的用量限额。
  • Memory 与上下文窗口是分开的。Claude 在你聊天的过程中把记忆保存为一组独立的主题，而不是在对话结束后总结对话，并把这些上下文带入新的对话 —— 有了记忆，Claude 就从一个无状态的聊天界面转变为一个随着时间推移不断加深理解的、见多识广的协作者。你可以在设置（Settings）中查看、编辑或删除记忆主题，告诉 Claude「记住这个（remember this）」，或者为你的账户或某一次聊天关闭记忆。
  • Project 记忆是有范围的：每个 Project 都有它自己的记忆；在某个 Project 中工作时，Claude 会使用该 Project 的记忆以及你来自 Project 之外聊天的记忆，但不会使用其他 Projects 的记忆。
  • 持久保存与重启：持久的上下文应当放在一个 Project 的知识库与指令中，或放在记忆主题中；单次聊天的上下文窗口是临时的。无痕（incognito）聊天与关闭了记忆的聊天不会被保存到记忆中。

关键技能：

  • 判断何时重启：当话题改变、当对话已经跑偏，或当对早期细节的回忆开始滑落（上下文腐化）时，开始一次新的聊天。
  • 判断何时总结：让 Claude 给出一份简洁的交接总结，包含决定、事实与未决事项，然后把它粘贴到一次新聊天中，或保存到一个 Project 中，以便在不带累积包袱的情况下继续。
  • 判断什么要持久保存：把稳定的事实、偏好与参考文件放进记忆或一个 Project 的知识库与指令中，而不是依赖一次长对话来承载它们。
  • 定期检查并编辑已保存的记忆主题，使过时的事实（例如一个已经改期的截止日期）不会持续影响新的对话。

7.1 Identify, diagnose, and resolve issues with underperforming prompts or poor outputs

Official wording: "Language models can fabricate specific-looking details such as citation numbers, a hallucination.   • Optimization starts with fit: aligning an approach with task requirements means matching effort and capability to the work — the Exam Guide's example is using a faster, lower-cost model for straightforward, high-volume tasks and reserving the most capable model for complex reasoning, since always using the top model wastes the cost and latency budget.
  • Setup is an efficiency lever: a Project with instructions and knowledge sources removes repeated pasting and re-briefing, and connectors remove manual fetching from Gmail, Calendar, and Drive.
  • Connector loading is also a lever: when many connectors are active, switching Tool access from Auto to On demand gives conversations more room, which the Help Center recommends considering with 10 or more connectors active.
  • Large Projects stay effective through RAG, which expands project knowledge capacity by up to 10x on paid plans while maintaining response quality — but curated, current knowledge still outperforms a larger, stale pile.
  • Effectiveness is measured at the workflow level: total human time (prompting, verifying, revising), not just generation speed, is the cost to optimize.

Key skills:

  • Standardize recurring work: turn a proven prompt sequence into a Project with standing instructions, named knowledge sources, and a fixed output format.
  • Batch and template high-volume, low-variation tasks, and reserve open-ended chat for genuinely exploratory work.
  • Periodically audit a workflow: remove connectors and files that are no longer used, tighten instructions that cause repeated revisions, and re-check that the model and approach still match the task's quality, efficiency, and cost requirements.

7.3 优化工作流的效率与效果

EN: Optimize workflows for efficiency and effectiveness

官方原文： "Select appropriate approaches to balance quality, efficiency, and cost" — Claude Certified Associate – Foundations Exam Guide v1.0, Anthropic (official exam guide)

关键知识：

  • 优化从契合开始：让一种方法与任务要求相匹配，意味着让投入的精力与能力与工作相称 —— 考试指南的例子是，对简单、高量的任务使用更快的、低成本的模型，把最强大的模型留给复杂推理，因为始终使用顶级模型会浪费成本与延迟预算。
  • 设置是一个效率杠杆：一个带指令与知识来源的 Project 消除了反复的粘贴与重新简报，connectors 消除了从 Gmail、Calendar 与 Drive 中人工取数的工作。
  • Connector 的加载也是一个杠杆：当许多 connectors 处于活动状态时，把工具访问（Tool access）从自动（Auto）切换到按需（On demand），能为对话腾出更多空间；帮助中心建议在有 10 个或更多 connectors 处于活动状态时考虑这样做。
  • 大型 Projects 通过 RAG 保持有效性，RAG 在付费方案上把项目知识容量扩大至多 10 倍，同时保持回答质量 —— 但经过整理、保持最新的知识仍然胜过一个更大、更陈旧的堆积。
  • 效果是在工作流层面衡量的：要优化的成本是人类的总时间（提示、核实、修订），而不只是生成的速度。

关键技能：

  • 把反复出现的工作标准化：把一个被证明有效的提示序列变成一个带常设指令、具名知识来源与固定输出格式的 Project。
  • 对高量、低变化的任务进行批处理并使用模板，把开放式聊天留给真正探索性的工作。
  • 定期审计一个工作流：删除不再使用的 connectors 与文件，收紧导致反复修订的指令，并重新核查模型与方法是否仍然符合任务在质量、效率与成本方面的要求。

Sample 1 · Domain 2 — Output Evaluation and Validation

An associate asks Claude to summarize a new regulation, and Claude produces a confident summary citing a specific subsection number. Before sending the summary to the compliance team, what is the most appropriate action?

Self-reported confidence (A, C) is not a reliable accuracy signal, and reformatting (D) does not address correctness.

Sample 2 · Domain 3 — Product and Model Selection

An associate needs to generate a high volume of short customer-reply drafts where speed and cost matter more than deep reasoning. Which choice best fits the task?

  • A. Use the most capable, highest-cost model for every reply to maximize quality.
  • B. Use a faster, lower-cost model suited to straightforward, high-volume tasks.
  • C. Disable all product features to reduce cost.
  • D. Switch to a different AI platform.

Answer: B. Aligning model selection with task requirements means matching a faster, lower-cost model to straightforward, high-volume work, reserving the most capable model for complex reasoning. Sample 3 · Domain 6 — Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use

A project manager wants to upload a spreadsheet containing customer names and account numbers so Claude can analyze trends. Organizational policy restricts sharing regulated personal data. What is the most appropriate action?

  • A. Upload the file as-is, since the analysis is internal.
  • B. Remove or anonymize the personal identifiers before uploading, consistent with policy.
  • C. Upload the file but instruct Claude not to retain it.
  • D. Skip the analysis entirely.

Answer: B. Applying data-sensitivity and privacy safeguards means redacting or anonymizing regulated identifiers before use, so the analysis can proceed without exposing protected data. Uploading as-is (A) violates policy; instructing the model not to retain data (C) does not satisfy the policy control; abandoning the task (D) is unnecessary when anonymization enables it.

样题 1 · 领域 2 —— 输出评估与验证（Sample 1 · Domain 2 — Output Evaluation and Validation）

一位助理级人员让 Claude 总结一项新法规，Claude 产出了一份自信的摘要，并引用了一个具体的小节编号。在把这份摘要发送给合规团队之前，最合适的行动是什么？

  • A. 直接按原样发送，因为 Claude 表达了高度自信。
  • B. 在分享之前，对照官方的法规文本核实所引用的小节。
  • C. 让 Claude 给自己的自信打分，如果分数高就发送。
  • D. 把摘要改写得更正式，然后发送。

答案：B。语言模型可能编造看似具体的细节，例如引用编号 —— 这是一种幻觉。对照权威来源验证事实性论断，尤其是面向合规受众的引用，是所要求的审慎步骤。自我报告的自信（A、C）不是可靠的准确性信号，重新排版（D）并不解决正确性问题。

样题 2 · 领域 3 —— 产品与模型选择（Sample 2 · Domain 3 — Product and Model Selection）

一位助理级人员需要生成大量简短的客户回复草稿，其中速度与成本比深度推理更重要。哪个选择最适合这项任务？

  • A. 对每一份回复都使用最强大、成本最高的模型，以最大化质量。
  • B. 使用一个更快的、低成本的、适合简单高量任务的模型。
  • C. 禁用所有产品功能以降低成本。
  • D. 切换到另一个 AI 平台。

答案：B。让模型选择与任务要求相匹配，意味着把一个更快的、低成本的模型用于简单、高量的工作，把最强大的模型留给复杂推理。始终使用顶级模型（A）会浪费成本与延迟预算；禁用功能（C）或切换平台（D）并不解决这一权衡问题。

样题 3 · 领域 6 —— 治理、风险与负责任使用（Sample 3 · Domain 6 — Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use）

一位项目经理想上传一份包含客户姓名与账号的电子表格，让 Claude 分析趋势。组织政策限制分享受监管的个人数据。最合适的行动是什么？

  • A. 按原样上传文件，因为这项分析是内部的。
  • B. 在上传之前删除或匿名化个人标识符，符合政策要求。
  • C. 上传文件，但指示 Claude 不要保留它。
  • D. 完全跳过这项分析。

答案：B。运用数据敏感性与隐私保障措施，意味着在使用之前对受监管的标识符进行脱敏或匿名化，使分析可以在不暴露受保护数据的情况下进行。按原样上传（A）违反政策；指示模型不要保留数据（C）并不能满足政策控制要求；而当匿名化能使任务得以进行时，放弃任务（D）是没有必要的。
